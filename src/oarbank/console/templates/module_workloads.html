A workload is a long-running process of this module that is the work itself. It runs only on the nodes you assign it to, sandboxed like the module's jobs, and yields to your protected processes: throttled, lowered, paused for at most the time it tolerates, then stopped and held while the cause lasts. A workload with a per-node identity runs under a seat lease, so one identity never runs on two nodes at once.
