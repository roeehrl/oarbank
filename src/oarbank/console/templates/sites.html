Sites

A site is the nodes that share one internet line. Large transfers from two of them at once slow each other down, and a protected program on one of them may have to upload on time. While a protected process at a site is in a phase its rule tags uplink (or that phase cannot be read, or its node stops reporting), network-heavy fleet work pauses on every node of the site: dataset staging, uploads, checkpoints, releases and jobs whose stage declares network = "heavy". Outside such phases the site's budget limits how many of them run at once. A node is at one site at most.
